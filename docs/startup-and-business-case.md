# ProfessorSTEM — Startup & Business Case

> AI-powered, adaptive STEM education for K-12 students, powered by Google Gemini.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

ProfessorSTEM is an AI-powered STEM education platform for K-12 learners (ages 5–18, grades 1–12). It delivers personalized, adaptive learning experiences by combining Google Gemini AI with a structured assessment flow: an initial 25-question proficiency exam, a tailored study plan and learning guide, a final assessment, and a before/after progress comparison with actionable recommendations. Every quiz question is generated with age-appropriate language and is grade/school-level aware, with a deliberate difficulty mix (~33% recall, ~33% application, ~34% analysis).

The core value proposition is **measurable, personalized learning**: instead of static content, each student receives quizzes and study material generated for their age, grade, and demonstrated proficiency level, and the platform quantifies improvement between the initial and final assessments. Proficiency is classified as `high`, `intermediate`, or `low` based on both accuracy and speed, and recommendations adapt accordingly (Core Concepts Review, Practice More, or Advanced Learning).

The target market spans three audiences: K-12 students who want engaging, self-paced STEM practice; teachers and schools seeking to reduce assessment workload; and parents who want visible evidence of their child's progress. The platform is built on a production-grade Flask backend deployed via Vercel, with Supabase PostgreSQL for data storage, structured JSON logging, and CI/CD with automated quality gates.

## 2. Company Overview & Mission

**Mission:** Make high-quality, personalized STEM education accessible to every K-12 student by using AI to adapt teaching to each learner — not the other way around.

### The Problem

- **One-size-fits-all education.** Traditional classrooms and static e-learning content deliver the same material to every student regardless of age, grade level, or current understanding. Advanced students are bored; struggling students are left behind.
- **Teacher workload.** Writing, grading, and differentiating assessments consumes significant teacher time that could be spent actually teaching.
- **Lack of personalized feedback.** Most tools report a score but not *what to do next*. Students and parents rarely get concrete, improvement-oriented guidance.

### The Solution

ProfessorSTEM automates the assessment–instruction–reassessment loop:

1. Generate an age-appropriate, grade-aware diagnostic exam with AI.
2. Produce a personalized study plan and learning guide tailored to the student's age, grade, and school level.
3. Re-assess with a final exam and measure improvement.
4. Deliver specific recommendations based on the measured change (review core concepts, practice more, or advance to harder material).

## 3. Product & Feature Set

### (a) AI-Driven Initial Proficiency Assessment

The learning journey begins with a 25-question multiple-choice exam generated by the `CentralAgent` (`generate_quiz` / `parse_quiz` in `agents.py`). Questions are:

- Age-appropriate in language and framing
- Grade/school-level aware
- Balanced across difficulty: ~33% recall, ~33% application, ~34% analysis
- Validated on parse: if fewer than 10 questions are successfully parsed, the system retries with a backup agent

### (b) Personalized Study Plan & Learning Guide

After the first quiz results, the `study_plan` route (`routes.py`) generates a personalized study plan plus a full learning guide rendered as HTML, tailored to the student's age, grade, and school level. A loading spinner (via `session['loading']`) provides feedback while the AI generates content.

### (c) Final Assessment & Progress Comparison

The flow concludes with a final quiz (`final_quiz` → `submit_final_quiz` → `final_results`), which is scored and passed to proficiency assessment. The `comparison` route compares initial vs. final scores, computes the improvement, and generates improvement-based recommendations:

| Improvement | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Negative | Core Concepts Review |
| 0–9 points | Practice More |
| ≥ 10 points | Advanced Learning |

### (d) Proficiency Classification

`assess_proficiency(correct_count, total_questions, total_time)` in `proficiency.py` classifies performance as `high`, `intermediate`, or `low`:

- **high:** ≥ 80% correct and fast completion
- **low:** < 50% correct or very slow completion
- **intermediate:** everything in between

A deterministic fallback classification is used if the Gemini call fails, so the feature never breaks.

### (e) Authentication with Role-Based Access

`auth.py` provides signup, signin, and logout with **bcrypt** password hashing, backed by Supabase via `db.py` (`get_db_connection`). The **first registered user becomes an admin**; subsequent users are standard users. Role-based dashboards are served at `/admin/dashboard` and `/user/dashboard`.

### (f) Built-In Todo List

The `/todolist` route in `app.py` provides a built-in todo list so students can organize their learning goals alongside their study plan.

### (g) Multi-Subject Support: Math & Science

`config_agent.py` loads three separate Gemini API keys — `CENTRAL_API_KEY`, `MATH_API_KEY`, and `SCIENCE_API_KEY` — enabling dedicated Math and Science subject agents alongside the central agent. This architecture makes adding further subjects straightforward.

## 4. Target Market & Audience

### Primary: K-12 Students (Ages 5–18, Grades 1–12)

| Segment | Age Range | Grades | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elementary School | 5–11 | 1–6 | Simple language, engaging practice, foundational skills |
| Middle School | 11–14 | 6–9 | Concept reinforcement, exam preparation |
| High School | 14–18 | 9–12 | Advanced material, measurable progress toward exams |

Because quiz language and difficulty are generated age- and grade-aware, a single platform serves the full K-12 range.

### Secondary: Teachers & Institutions

Schools and teachers benefit from automated assessment generation and grading, reducing workload while gaining insight into individual student proficiency.

### Tertiary: Parents & Education Administrators

Parents want visible, quantified evidence of improvement (initial vs. final score comparison). Administrators benefit from role-based dashboards and structured, auditable data.

## 5. Business Model & Value Proposition

### Value Proposition

- **For students:** Personalized quizzes, study plans, and learning guides matched to their age and level — plus a clear measure of how much they improved.
- **For institutions:** Reduced teacher workload for assessment creation and grading; consistent, difficulty-balanced exams; role-based access for administration.
- **For parents:** Objective before/after progress measurement and concrete recommendations for what their child should do next.

## 6. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor type | Typical offering | ProfessorSTEM differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Generic human tutoring | High cost, variable quality, scheduling friction | AI-generated, on-demand, difficulty-balanced assessments at scale |
| Static e-learning platforms | Fixed question banks, same content for everyone | Fully adaptive: age-appropriate, grade-aware generated content |
| Other AI learning tools | Often single-subject or single-age-band, score-only feedback | Multi-subject (Math & Science), full K-12 range, and **measurable before/after progress** with actionable recommendations |

**Key differentiators:**

1. **Adaptive & age-appropriate** — every quiz and study guide is generated for the learner's age, grade, and school level.
2. **Multi-subject architecture** — dedicated Math and Science agents via separate API keys.
3. **Measurable outcomes** — initial vs. final score comparison with improvement-based recommendations, not just a score.
4. **Resilient AI pipeline** — retry with backup agents and deterministic fallbacks keep the product working even when AI calls fail.

## 7. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phased Rollout

1. **Phase 1 — School pilots:** Partner with a small number of schools to run the assessment → study plan → reassessment loop in real classrooms and gather outcome data.
2. **Phase 2 — Regional expansion:** Expand within the pilot regions using collected improvement evidence as the sales case.
3. **Phase 3 — National scale:** Broad distribution through institutional licensing and self-serve signups.

### Channels

- **School partnerships** — direct pilots and district-level agreements (primary channel).
- **Parent word-of-mouth** — the before/after progress report is a naturally shareable proof point.
- **Ed-tech marketplaces** — listing for self-serve discovery by teachers and parents.

## 9. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **AI content accuracy** | Generated quiz questions or study plans may contain errors. | Difficulty-mix validation, parse validation with retry via backup agent (`agents.py`), and deterministic fallbacks. Human review in school pilots. |
| **Data privacy (student data)** | Handling minors' data requires strict controls. | Supabase PostgreSQL with **Row Level Security enabled** and policies "Users can view own data" / "Users can update own data" (`auth.uid() = id`) in `migrations/001_initial_schema.sql`; bcrypt password hashing; security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options). |
| **API cost & availability** | Gemini API outages or cost spikes could degrade service. | Existing retry/fallback logic: backup-agent retry in `agents.py` and deterministic proficiency fallback in `proficiency.py`; separate API keys per subject allow independent scaling/budgeting. |
| **Market adoption** | Schools have long procurement cycles. | Phased pilot-led GTM; measurable before/after improvement reports as the proof point; freemium entry tier (proposed) to lower adoption friction. |

## 10. Financial Overview (Indicative)

> All figures in this section are **estimates/assumptions**, not audited financials.

### Cost Drivers

| Cost driver | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Gemini API usage** | Scales with quiz/study-plan generation volume; three separate keys (central, math, science) allow per-subject budget tracking |
| **Supabase hosting** | PostgreSQL database, auth, and RLS; scales with user count and storage tier |
| **Vercel / infrastructure** | Flask backend deployment (`vercel.json`), bandwidth, and serverless execution |

### Revenue Assumptions (Proposals)

- **Freemium conversion:** a small percentage of free users converting to a paid individual tier.
- **Institutional licensing:** annual per-institution fees as the primary revenue driver at scale, with per-seat pricing for larger districts.
- **Unit economics:** gross margin driven by the ratio of subscription/licensing revenue to variable Gemini API + hosting costs per active student; the retry/fallback design limits wasted API spend.

## 11. Appendix: Technical Architecture Summary

| Layer | Technology | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Backend** | Flask (`app.py`, `routes.py`, `wsgi.py`) | Route flow: `user_training → start_learning → first_quiz → submit_first_quiz → first_results → study_plan → final_quiz → submit_final_quiz → final_results → comparison` |

| **AI** | Google Gemini | `gemini-1.5-flash` for quiz/study-plan generation; `gemini-2.0-pro-exp-02-05` for proficiency assessment (`proficiency.py`); `CentralAgent` with `math_agent` / `science_agent` SubjectAgents (`agents.py`); keys via `config_agent.py` |

| **Operations** | `docs/database-backup.md` | Backup / point-in-time recovery (PITR) runbook |
